Terms, Rules & Regulations

1. Nature of Contribution (Voluntary)

All contributions made through yous2.in are voluntary micro-contributions meant exclusively for supporting medical treatments, poverty relief, and humanitarian services managed under YouS2 Seba Mission. No commercial goods or services are promised in exchange.

2. Payment Terms & Non-Refund Policy

Payments are processed securely via Razorpay. Since funds are pooled and immediately directed toward charitable activities and emergency social work, contributions once made are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.

3. Transparency & Fund Utilization

YouS2 Seba Mission ensures that collected funds are used responsibly. Detailed records, impact metrics, and recipient updates are maintained. Donors acknowledge that management holds the authority to allocate funds based on immediate urgency of cases.

4. User Data & Privacy Protection

Personal details (Name, Phone, Email) collected during payment are stored safely in our encrypted Google Sheet ledger to maintain contribution records and send transparency reports. We never sell or share donor data with third parties.
