Terms & Conditions

Welcome to YouS2 Seba Mission (yous2.in). By accessing our website and contributing to our community welfare initiatives, you agree to comply with the following:

  • No Cash Donations: We do not ask for or accept any monetary, digital cash, or bank transfers. All contributions are in-kind physical items only.
  • Direct Distribution: All contributed clothes, food packets, blankets, and essentials are directly handed over to street children and underprivileged families.
  • Transparent Operation: We maintain complete photographic and inventory records of every distributed item.

Privacy Policy

At YouS2 Seba Mission, we respect your privacy and protect your personal data:

  • Data Collection: We collect your Name, Phone Number, and Address solely for coordinating item pickups and maintaining delivery ledgers.
  • Data Security: Your data is stored securely and never sold or shared with third-party commercial marketing entities.
  • Contact: For any questions, you can reach out directly to Subrata Mazumdar at our Baguiati office or via WhatsApp.
