Terms & Conditions

Welcome to YouS2 Seba Mission (yous2.in). By accessing our website and contributing to our community welfare initiatives, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions:

  • Voluntary Contribution: All contributions made through our platform are completely voluntary micro-contributions intended for medical aid, food distribution, and poverty relief.
  • Non-Refundable Policy: Since funds are pooled and immediately deployed toward emergency social relief and medical purchases, contributions once made are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.
  • Transparent Fund Allocation: Management holds the authority to allocate funds based on the urgency of medical and humanitarian cases.

Privacy Policy

At YouS2 Seba Mission, we respect your privacy and protect your personal data. This policy outlines how we collect and manage your information:

  • Data Collection: We collect your Name, Phone Number, and Email Address solely for issuing digital certificates, payment receipts, and maintaining transparency ledgers.
  • Data Security: Your data is stored securely in encrypted Google Sheets and is never sold, traded, or rented to third-party commercial entities.
  • Contact: For any privacy concerns, you can reach out directly to Subrata Mazumdar at our Baguiati office or via WhatsApp.
